# SpoX privacy policy

SpoX runs on your own computer and has no server of its own. It collects nothing about you.

- **Your keys and Spotify sign-in** are saved only on your PC (in `%APPDATA%\SpoX\config.json`). They go only to Spotify, to play music and read your playlists.
- **Lyrics** are looked up on LRCLIB (lrclib.net) by song title and artist.
- **New-song suggestions** come from Last.fm, only if you add your own Last.fm key.
- **Discord:** if you turn on "Show song on Discord", SpoX tells the Discord app on your PC which song is playing, so it can show it on your profile. Turn it off any time in Settings.
- SpoX has no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

To remove everything, uninstall SpoX and say yes when it asks whether to delete your settings.
